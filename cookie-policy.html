Cookie Policy

What cookies Stingray Manor uses and how to control them.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Stingray Manor uses cookies and similar technologies on www.stingraymanor.com. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your preferences and help owners understand how their pages are used. Some are set by us (first-party) and others by services we use (third-party).

Cookies we use

Essential

These remember basic choices, such as your response to our cookie notice, using your browser’s local storage. They are necessary for the website to function and do not track you elsewhere.

Analytics

Google Analytics sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to collect aggregated information about visits, popular pages and navigation. These usually last up to two years and help us improve our guides.

Advertising

If ads appear on the website, Google and other vendors may set cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other sites, limit repetition and measure performance. Manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings.

Managing cookies

You can accept all cookies or keep to essential ones using our cookie notice. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website features.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Updates

We may update this policy if our tools or legal requirements change. Check the date above for the latest version.
