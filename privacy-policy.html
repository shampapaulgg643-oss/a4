Privacy Policy

How Stingray Manor collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Stingray Manor (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates www.stingraymanor.com, an independent editorial guide to luxury handbags. We respect your privacy and collect only what we need to run the website and respond to you. This policy explains how.

Information you provide

When you use our contact form we receive your name, email address, selected topic and message. When you subscribe to The Manor Letter we receive your email address. We do not sell products and never request payment details, passwords or identity documents.

Information collected automatically

Our servers and analytics tools automatically record technical details such as IP address, browser type, device, operating system, referring website, pages visited and time on page. Much of this is collected through cookies, explained in our Cookie Policy.

How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiries.
  • To send The Manor Letter to subscribers.
  • To understand which guides readers find useful and improve the website.
  • To protect the website from spam, fraud and abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure website traffic and usage. It uses cookies to collect information such as the pages you view and how long you stay, which Google processes under its own policies, described at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You may opt out using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

This website may show advertisements served by Google and other third-party vendors. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with trusted providers that help us operate the website, such as hosting and email services, under appropriate confidentiality obligations, or where the law requires disclosure.

Retention

We keep enquiry emails only as long as necessary to deal with them. Newsletter subscriptions are stored until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained in line with our Google Analytics settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and CCPA, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link.

Children

This website is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children and will delete it if we learn that we have.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS encryption, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
