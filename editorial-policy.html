Editorial Policy

How we research, write and correct our handbag guides.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Readers come to Stingray Manor for trustworthy information about fine handbags. These are the standards we follow when researching, writing and maintaining our guides.

Independence

We do not accept payment, gifts or free products in exchange for coverage. We are not affiliated with any brand, and we do not name or promote specific labels in our guides. If advertising appears on the website, advertisers have no influence over what we write.

Research

Our guides are based on established knowledge of leather production and finishing, practical experience examining bags, and conversations with leatherworkers and repair specialists. Where practices vary or experts disagree, we explain the range of views rather than presenting one as fact.

Responsible topics

When writing about exotic materials such as stingray leather, we include information about origin and regulation and encourage responsible buying. We avoid exaggerated claims about value, resale prices or rarity.

Accuracy and corrections

We review our guides regularly. If you notice an error, please contact us through our contact page. We investigate every report and correct confirmed mistakes promptly, updating the page date when a meaningful change is made.

Images

Images are sourced from Unsplash under the Unsplash License and chosen to illustrate styles and materials honestly. We describe them with accurate alternative text and avoid images featuring prominent brand logos.
