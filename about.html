About us

About Stingray Manor

An independent editorial guide for people who love beautifully made handbags and want to understand them more deeply.

Our story

A house built on curiosity

Stingray Manor began with a single bag: a small shagreen clutch found in a vintage shop, its beaded surface still shimmering after decades. Nobody in the shop could explain what it was made of or how to care for it. Finding out took weeks of reading, visits to repair workshops and conversations with leatherworkers.

Those notes became this website. Our aim is simple: to share clear, honest knowledge about the materials and craft behind fine handbags, so readers can make confident choices and look after what they already own.

Independent

We do not sell bags and have no affiliation with any brand or fashion house. We are free to write what we believe.

Craft first

We care more about stitching, leather and construction than logos, trends or hype.

Longevity

We encourage buying thoughtfully, caring well and repairing rather than replacing.

How we work

Research you can rely on

Our guides draw on established knowledge of leather tanning and finishing, conversations with experienced leatherworkers and repairers, and hands-on examination of bags new and old. Where information is uncertain or disputed, we say so.

We never publish paid reviews, and advertising on the site, if shown, has no influence on our writing. You can read more in our Editorial Policy.

A note on names

Why you won’t see brand logos here

We deliberately keep our guides focused on materials and techniques rather than specific labels. It keeps our advice useful whatever your budget, and it avoids any suggestion of endorsement or affiliation.

Photographs on this site come from Unsplash and are used to illustrate styles and materials. They do not represent products for sale.
